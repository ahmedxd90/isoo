Room chat clear realtime update — 2026-09-06

The room chat clear action now deletes all room_messages through the existing owner/moderator RPC and publishes a Supabase Realtime Broadcast event named clear on room-chat:<room_id>. Every connected room client clears its visible messages immediately and filters out messages created before the clear timestamp, while the database deletion remains the source of truth. The channel is removed when leaving the room.
